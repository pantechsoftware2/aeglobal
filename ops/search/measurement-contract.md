# Measurement Contract

The initial implementation can be verified by build output and rendered HTML after deployment. Search and business outcomes require external access and time.

## Metrics Requiring Access

- Google Search Console: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, query/page pairs and crawl/index reports.
- Analytics or CRM: enquiry submissions, lead source, qualified leads and conversion rate.
- Hosting: deployment ID, production branch, rollback target and live logs.
- AI visibility: saved neutral prompt samples from specified products and settings.

## Reporting Rules

- Do not treat implementation as an indexing or ranking win.
- Do not infer query/page performance without a joint query/page export.
- Do not count direct URL retrieval prompts as neutral AI discovery.
- Use complete comparable windows for before/after analysis once data is available.
