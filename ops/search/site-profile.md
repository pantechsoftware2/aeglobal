# Site Profile

Observed on 2026-09-30.

## Business

AE Global Group provides study abroad counseling for students comparing destinations, institutions, applications, visa preparation, accommodation and pre-departure planning.

## Audience

Students and families planning international study. The site currently speaks in English and emphasizes clear options, careful preparation and practical guidance.

## Conversion Path

The primary conversion is a student enquiry through the lead form. Secondary conversion paths are contact page phone/email links, "I'm Ready to Start", "Talk to an advisor", and destination-specific enquiry buttons.
